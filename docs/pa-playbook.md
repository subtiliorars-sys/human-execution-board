# PA Playbook — Step-by-Step Execution Guides

This playbook is written so anyone can follow it. No jargon. Just steps.

## 📖 Publishing Playbook: Sell an Ebook in One Day

**Time:** 2 hours (first time), 30 minutes after (you'll get faster)  
**Platforms:** Gumroad, Amazon KDP, Leanpub  
**Goal:** Take a finished guide/course/playbook and get it selling on 3 platforms

### What You'll Need

- A finished document (Word, Google Docs, or Markdown file)
- A cover image (just a simple rectangle, doesn't need to be fancy)
- A title and description
- Price (we recommend $9–29)

### Step 1: Prepare the Document (15 min)

#### Option A: If it's a Google Doc
1. Open the doc
2. Click `File` → `Download` → `PDF Document (.pdf)`
3. Save it to your Desktop with a simple name (e.g., "PrivateScribe.pdf")
4. Close the dialog

#### Option B: If it's a Word file
1. Open the file in Word
2. Click `File` → `Save As`
3. Choose "PDF" from the dropdown
4. Click `Save`

#### Option C: If it's a Markdown file (like a GitHub doc)
1. Open it in any text editor (Notepad works)
2. Copy all the text
3. Paste into Google Docs
4. Follow Option A above

**Check:** You should now have a `.pdf` file on your Desktop. Double-click it to make sure it looks right (no broken images, all pages are there).

### Step 2: Create a Cover Image (5–10 min)

We're not making a fancy cover — just a simple rectangle that shows the title.

1. Open Canva (canva.com) — you may need to log in or create a free account
2. Search for "book cover" in the templates
3. Pick ANY template you like (they all work)
4. Click into the template to edit it
5. Change the title to your book title
6. Delete or hide the author name if you want
7. Keep the colors simple (don't overthink this)
8. Click `Download` in the top right
9. Choose "PNG" format and click `Download`
10. Save it to your Desktop

**Check:** You should have a cover image file (a `.png`) on your Desktop. Open it to make sure the title is readable.

### Step 3: Upload to Gumroad (15 min)

Gumroad is the easiest platform to start with.

1. Go to gumroad.com
2. If you don't have an account, click "Sign up" and use your email
3. Log in
4. Click your profile icon in the top right → "Create a product"
5. Fill in:
   - **Product Name:** (e.g., "Private Scribe: How to Write Sales Copy")
   - **Description:** Write 2–3 sentences about what the reader will learn. Example: "This guide walks you through the 5-part copywriting framework we use to write landing pages that convert. No fluff. Just the step-by-step system."
   - **Price:** (e.g., $19)
   - **Product Type:** "Digital Product"
6. Scroll down and click "Add file" → choose your `.pdf` file from your Desktop
7. Scroll down and click "Add cover image" → choose your `.png` cover from your Desktop
8. At the bottom, click "Publish"
9. You'll get a link (like `gumroad.com/l/xxxxx`). Copy it and save it somewhere — you'll use it for the other platforms.

**Check:** Click your Gumroad link. A real product page should load, and you should be able to see the cover, title, description, and price.

### Step 4: Upload to Amazon KDP (30 min)

KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) reaches millions of readers. Don't skip this one.

1. Go to kdp.amazon.com
2. Log in with your Amazon account (if you don't have one, create one — it's free)
3. Click "Create a new Kindle eBook" (or "Create Kindle eBook")
4. Fill in:
   - **Book Title:** (same as Gumroad)
   - **Author Name:** (your name or the business name)
   - **Description:** (same as Gumroad)
   - **Language:** English
   - **Publisher Name:** (leave blank or put "subtiliorars-sys")
5. Click "Next"
6. Upload your cover:
   - Click "Upload cover"
   - Choose your `.png` file from Desktop
7. Upload your manuscript:
   - Click "Upload manuscript"
   - Choose your `.pdf` file from Desktop
   - Confirm the formatting looks right in the preview (it should)
8. Set your price:
   - Click the price dropdown
   - Enter your price (e.g., $19)
   - Leave royalty at "70%" (the default)
9. Click "Publish"
   - A popup asks about territories — just click "Worldwide rights" (the default)
10. You're done. Amazon will send you a link in a few hours, and the book goes live in 24–48 hours.

**Check:** Save the Amazon order ID they give you. In 24 hours, go back to kdp.amazon.com and check that your book appears in your bookshelf and is marked "Live."

### Step 5: Upload to Leanpub (15 min)

Leanpub is great because readers get updates automatically if you improve the book later.

1. Go to leanpub.com
2. Log in or sign up
3. Click "Publish a new book"
4. Fill in:
   - **Book Title:** (same as above)
   - **Author:** (your name)
   - **Description:** (same as above)
5. Scroll down to "Upload manuscript"
6. Choose your `.pdf` file
7. Scroll to "Pricing"
   - Set your suggested price (e.g., $19)
   - Set a minimum price (e.g., $9 — this is the lowest price readers can pay, but they can choose to pay more)
8. Click "Publish"

**Check:** Click the link they give you. Your book should be live within minutes.

### Step 6: Update Your Links Everywhere (5 min)

Now that your book is live on 3 platforms, tell people where to find it.

1. Add a row to your products spreadsheet (or just keep a list) with:
   - Book title
   - Gumroad link
   - Amazon KDP link
   - Leanpub link
   - Price on each
   - Date published
2. If you have a website or social media, add the links there

**Check:** Send the links to a friend and ask them to click one. It should work and show your real book page.

### Done ✅

You've published to 3 platforms. Every time someone buys, money lands in your account automatically (Gumroad and Leanpub wire you weekly or monthly; Amazon every 30 days).

Next book? Repeat these steps. You'll finish in 30 minutes.

## 📧 Outreach Playbook: Contact a Prospect

**Time:** 5–15 minutes per prospect (depending on channel)  
**Platforms:** Phone, email, walk-in  
**Goal:** Make first contact with a business prospect

### Before You Start

You'll have a list of business names, phone numbers, and/or emails. Each one has:
- A **vertical kit** (template for restaurants, auto shops, retail, etc.)
- A **contact name** (if we have one)
- A **phone number** and/or **email**
- An **angle** (one-line reason they'd want to talk to us)

### Phone Call (First Time Scary? Read This First)

**Script:** It's three sentences.

> "Hi, is this [Name]? This is [Your Name] with OmniTender — I help [restaurants / shops] in Burke County see what you're actually paying in card processing fees. I have a free 15-minute analysis that usually saves people money. Do you have 15 minutes this week?"

**That's it.** Say those three sentences, then listen.

#### Step-by-Step: Making the Call

1. Look at the prospect name and phone number
2. Read the script above out loud twice (yes, really — it sounds less robotic)
3. Pick up the phone and dial the number
4. When someone answers, say: "Hi, is this [Name]?" (use the contact name from the list if you have one)
5. If it's them, say your three-sentence script (read it off paper if you need to)
6. If they say "not interested," say "No problem, thanks for your time" and hang up (mark as "Dead" in the tracker)
7. If they say "yes, but not now," say "Perfect, when would work better — this week or next?" and write down the callback date
8. If they say "yes," stop talking. Ask: "What's your email?" (write it down). Then hang up.

**After the call:** Update the outreach tracker:
- Change Status to "Contacted"
- Write the date and time
- If they said yes, note their email address
- If they want you to call back, write the date

### Email (When There's No Phone Number)

**Subject line:** "[Business Name] — quick processing fee check?"

**Email text:**
> Hi [Name],
> 
> I'm [Your Name] with OmniTender, local to Burke County. I help [restaurants / retailers / auto shops] see what they're actually paying in card fees — batch, PCI, and per-transaction charges most statements don't show.
> 
> Free 15-minute call this week? I'll walk through what a rate analysis looks like — no statements needed yet, no commitment.
> 
> [Your Phone Number]  
> OmniTender | (828) 413-0859
> 
> Reply STOP to opt out.

#### Step-by-Step: Sending an Email

1. Open Gmail (or your email client)
2. Click "Compose" or "New"
3. In the **To:** field, paste the prospect's email address
4. In the **Subject:** field, type: "[Business Name] — quick processing fee check?"
   - Replace [Business Name] with the actual name (e.g., "Mountain Burrito — quick processing fee check?")
5. Click in the message box and type the email above
   - Replace [Your Name] with your actual name
   - Replace [restaurants / retailers / auto shops] with the correct type for this prospect (use the vertical kit)
6. Copy your phone number into the signature
7. Click "Send"

**After the email:** Update the outreach tracker:
- Change Status to "Contacted"
- Write the date
- Log "Email" in the channel column

### Walk-In (For Restaurants with No Phone)

Sometimes you have an address but no phone number. Just show up.

#### Step-by-Step: Walk-In Visit

1. Look up the address on Google Maps (make sure they're open)
2. Drive or walk there
3. Go inside, find someone who looks like they work there (not a customer)
4. Say your script (the same three sentences as the phone call, but friendly)
5. If they're busy, say "I'll come back at [time]" and leave
6. If they're interested, ask for their email and/or business card

**After:** Update the outreach tracker:
- Change Status to "Contacted"
- Write the date
- Log "Walk-in" in the channel column

### What If They Say Yes?

Great! You've moved them to "Contacted" status. Next step (not your job, but good to know):
- Someone will send them a rate-analysis proposal (2–3 days)
- They'll review it
- You (or someone) will follow up in 48 hours to ask what they think

### What If They Say No?

Also fine. Log it and move on. They've been contacted; we update the record so we don't bother them again.

## 💰 Sales Playbook: Close a Lead

**Time:** 10–30 minutes  
**Goal:** Move a prospect from "Statement Requested" to "Verbal Yes" or closer to a deal

### The Funnel

Every prospect moves through these stages:
1. **Not contacted** ← (you did this with Outreach)
2. **Contacted** ← (you made first call/email)
3. **Statement requested** ← (they said "send me numbers")
4. **Analysis sent** ← (we sent a summary; waiting for reaction)
5. **Verbal yes** ← (they said "yeah, let's do it" — this is your goal)
6. **Onboarding** ← (paperwork in progress)
7. **Active** ← (making money)

### Your Job: Move People Forward

You're the follow-up person. When a prospect is stuck (waiting 48+ hours with no response), you push gently.

#### The 48-Hour Follow-Up

Someone sent them a proposal. If they haven't responded in 2 days, you call.

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] with OmniTender. I sent you a rate analysis a couple days ago — did you get a chance to look at it?"

If they say "not yet": "No problem, when would work for a quick call this week?"  
If they say "yes, but not interested": "Any reason why? Is it pricing, or something else?" (Listen. This tells us how to improve.)  
If they say "yes, I want to do it": "Awesome! Here's what happens next..." (and you tell them the onboarding process)

#### Step-by-Step: Making the 48-Hour Call

1. Check the outreach tracker — find prospects in "Analysis sent" status
2. If the "Last touch" date is 2+ days ago, they get a call
3. Use the script above
4. Write down what they say
5. Update the tracker:
   - If they said yes → change Status to "Verbal yes"
   - If they said no → change Status to "Dead"
   - If they want to wait → write the callback date

## 🤝 Partnership Playbook: Apply for a Partner Program

**Time:** 30–60 minutes  
**Goal:** Get approved by a payment processor or vendor

### Example: PaymentCloud Application

PaymentCloud lets merchants accept credit cards through us without needing their own setup.

#### Step-by-Step: Apply to PaymentCloud

1. Go to paymentcloud.com/partner
2. Click "Apply Now"
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Name:** (your business name or your name)
   - **Email:** (a business email you can check)
   - **Business Type:** "Sales Agent" or "MSO" (we're helping merchants, so pick "Sales Agent")
   - **Region:** (the area you serve — e.g., North Carolina)
   - **Monthly Volume:** (guess a reasonable number for your first deal — e.g., $50,000)
4. Submit
5. They'll email you in 1–3 business days

#### After They Approve You

1. They'll send you an approval email with:
   - Your partner ID
   - A sandbox (test) environment to try things
   - Links to their portal (where you see transactions, disputes, etc.)
2. Save all of this information in a shared doc with Daniel
3. Wait for Daniel to confirm the next steps (might be a meeting with them, might be starting to onboard merchants)

#### During the Approval Wait

- Don't tell prospects you're approved yet (you're not)
- Don't start any transactions
- Just wait for the email

## ✅ Approval Playbook: Get a Decision From Daniel

**Time:** 5 minutes (sending), up to 24 hours (getting a response)  
**Goal:** Get approval to move forward with an action

### When You Need an Approval

Examples:
- "Can I send the email campaign?" (decision)
- "Should we charge $19 or $29 for this ebook?" (decision)
- "This prospect wants to talk to you directly — should I set up a call?" (decision)

### How to Ask

Post in Slack or Discord (or email if Slack isn't set up yet):

> **[APPROVAL NEEDED]**
> 
> What: [What you want to do in one sentence]  
> Why: [Why now, and why this specific ask]  
> Options: [If there are multiple choices]  
> Deadline: [When you need the answer]
> 
> Examples:  
> "Option A: $19 / Option B: $29"  
> "Yes/No"  
> "Pick one: Send to all 20 prospects or start with 5?"

**Example of a full message:**
> **[APPROVAL NEEDED]**  
> What: Send the email outreach to 6 restaurant prospects  
> Why: They're ready (no statement yet, no prior contact), and we have capacity this week  
> Deadline: By EOD Thursday so I can send Friday morning  
> Daniel: Yes or No?

### What Happens Next

- Daniel will respond in the Slack/Discord message
- They'll say "Yes," "No," or "Let's modify and then do it"
- You do what they approved
- You update the board card and move it to Done
